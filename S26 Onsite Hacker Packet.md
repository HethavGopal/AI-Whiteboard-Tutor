# **Judging Information (Please read thoroughly\!)**

**Hackathon Deliverables (Due 8 am on April 19, 2026)**

1. You must submit a link to your project’s public GitHub repository created between April 17, 2026 and April 19, 2026\. Remember, hacking officially starts at 8pm on April 17, 2026\!  
2. You MUST enter into ONLY ONE HackPrinceton track. Choose one out of:  
   1. Healthcare  
   2. Sustainability  
   3. Business and Enterprise  
   4. Entertainment and Media  
   5. Education

   However, you are free to enter in as many Special Tracks as you desire.

3. You may submit an OPTIONAL (but highly recommended\!) video demo that does not exceed 2 minutes in length. Remember to leave time to upload your video to YouTube/any other video platform and add the video link to your Devpost submission. **IMPORTANT NOTE: In-person demos are REQUIRED on Sunday, regardless if you submitted a video or not\!**  
4. Judging will be from **9:30 AM to 2 PM on Sunday, April 19.** Hackers will be expected to present their finished work in front of the judges during this time period. You must prepare for a presentation explaining/demoing your project to the judges that **does not exceed 2 minutes** in length, even if you attached a video to your project. Your presentation should clearly indicate the problem your hack attempts to solve and a walkthrough of your hack’s functionality.  
5. Please add your Discord usernames to your submission so we can contact you if you win a prize\!

**Judging Criteria**

* Creativity  
  How unique is the concept behind the hack? Does it address a problem you didn't even know existed? Does it address a common problem in a creative way or with an intuitive algorithm? What is this hack’s “ah-ha” moment?  
* Utility  
  Does this hack function? If so, how in-depth is its functionality? How likely would you be to frequently use this hack in its current state in your own life?  
* Charity  
  Does this hack address a specific societal need? If so, how well does it address it? What contributions does this hack make in the nation’s service and in the service of humanity?  
* Avidity  
  How excited do the team members seem about the final state of their project? Passion can be demonstrated through their presentation, the project’s description on Devpost, the future plans they have for their hack, or an overall polished program.

**Judging Procedures**  
On Sunday morning, you and your team will be randomly assigned a number and a location in our venue. This assignment will be released on the Discord server shortly after the project submission deadline. Please note that your assigned numbers DO NOT indicate the order of judging.

Once you have received your numbers, please head to your assigned locations with your team and prepare for judging.

We usually get 80+ project submissions and we want each team to be judged by 3 different judges, so you might be waiting at your assigned location for a long time. We thank you in advance for your patience. 

There will be a lunch break in between the two judging rounds, and you may proceed to get lunch **ONLY AFTER YOU HAVE BEEN JUDGED 3 TIMES FOR THE HACKPRINCETON TRACKS** (i.e. Healthcare OR Sustainability OR Business & Enterprise OR Entertainment & Media OR Education).

During the first round of judging, judges from the Special Tracks will also be going around to judge their respective tracks. As a result of this, some teams might be visited by more than 3 judges. That is completely normal, and we ask that you do not worry. We promise we’ll get you three judges\!

As you enjoy your lunch, our team will aggregate all the scores and determine the finalists who will proceed to the second round of judging. We will announce these finalists by their team numbers on the Discord announcement channel. There will be no second round judging for Special Tracks– all judging for Special Tracks will conclude after the first round. Once a particular HackPrinceton track has announced its finalists, we will also announce that on the Discord as well.

# **FAQs**

**Q: What are HackPrinceton Tracks?**  
A: HackPrinceton tracks are what we consider our “main tracks”. By participating in HackPrinceton, you are required to submit into one of these tracks. If you’re not sure which track to participate in, you can simply choose the track that best describes your hack.

**Q: What are Special Tracks?**  
A: Special tracks are supported by companies. These tracks will be judged directly by company representatives. You are welcome to enroll in as many Special Tracks as you desire. Keep in mind, the more Special Tracks you enroll in, the more times you have to demo in front of judges.

**Q: Can I do more than one Special Track?**  
A: Yes\!

**Q: Where do I submit my project?**  
A: You can submit it at the HackPrinceton Devpost ([click to be redirected\!](https://hackprinceton-spring-26.devpost.com))

**Q: I don’t know anyone in the hackathon. How do I find a teammate?**  
A: There will be a team-building session right after the Opening Ceremony. We also recommend you use Discord to connect with other hackers and see if there’s anyone willing to team up. 

**Q: How many members can a team have?**  
A: Maximum of 4 members.

**Q: I prefer to work solo. Can I do that?**  
A: Yes\!

**Q: I have a team member who needs to come after Friday 5pm. Can they still check in?**  
A: Yes. Please inform an organizer, and we can check them in anytime after Friday 5 pm.

**Q: Can we use workshop rooms for hacking?**  
A: You are NOT allowed to use the workshop rooms for hacking during the listed workshop times seen in the schedule. Outside of these times, feel free to use the rooms. 

**Q: Will the opening ceremony slides be posted?**  
A: Yes, we will post them in the \#information channel on Discord.

**Q: Do you have any designated sleeping areas in the Hackathon?**  
A: Unfortunately, we are not allowed to designate sleeping areas around the venue. If you need to rest, we highly suggest finding refuge in one of the classrooms designated as a Hacker Space.

**Q: I need help with my hack. Where can I find help?**  
A: You can head into the mentor room to see if there’s a mentor available, or ping @Mentor at the Discord channel as well\!

**Q: We have a flight to catch, and need to leave before the Closing Ceremony. Can we do that?**  
A: While we highly recommend attending the Closing Ceremony, we understand that you have to leave, so you’re free to leave.

**Q: We weren’t called up for a second round of judging. Does this mean we won’t get any awards? Can we just skip the closing ceremony?**  
A: No\! Even if you aren’t called for a second round of judging, you might win a Special Track\! So we recommend that you stay for the Closing Ceremony.

**Q: I have some concerns about the hackathon/an incident I’d like to report. Where can I do that?**  
A: If your incident is an emergency and relates to either your safety or the safety of others in the venue, please find a Public Safety officer that’s on patrol. Otherwise, find one of the organizers on site or DM one of the organizers on Discord.
