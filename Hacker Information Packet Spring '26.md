* Best Overall Hack

Note: Each member of the team will receive one of these prizes. So if your team won Best Overall Hack and has 4 members, we will be giving your team 4 PS5s. If your team only has 3 members, then you will receive 3 PS5s.

**Judging will be from 9:30am-1:00 pm on Sunday, April 19th.** Hackers will be expected to present their finished work in front of the judges during this time period. You must prepare for a presentation explaining/demoing your project to the judges that does not exceed **2 minutes in length,** even if you attached a video to your project. Your presentation should clearly indicate the problem your hack attempts to solve and a walkthrough of your hack’s functionality.
